How it works: ACoS moves roughly in proportion to bid, so the ratio of target ACoS to current ACoS gives the factor your effective bid should move by. That factor is applied to your current effective bid (base bid × current adjustment) and converted back into a placement percentage. Amazon caps placement adjustments at 900%.

How it works: Profit before ads is price minus COGS, referral fee and fulfilment. Breakeven ACoS is that profit as a percentage of price — spend more than this on ads and the sale loses money. Max CPC is breakeven ACoS × price × conversion rate, because you pay for every click but only a fraction convert.

How it works: A term with no orders is only a loser once it has had a fair test — roughly twice the clicks implied by your conversion rate. Below that it is noise, not evidence. Above it, with no orders, the term is paying for traffic that does not buy. Where orders exist, the decision comes from ACoS against your target with a tolerance band, because trimming a bid is usually better than deleting the data.
