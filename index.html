How we work

A process, not improvisation.

Every account we take on follows the same sequence. You always know what is happening, why it is happening, and what is coming next.

01
Days 0–3 · Free of charge

We audit your account first

Before any agreement, before any invoice, we go through your campaigns, listings, keywords, catalogue and account health. You receive a written findings document showing exactly what is broken, what it is costing you, and what we would do about it. If we are not the right fit, you keep the audit anyway.

02
Days 3–7

We turn findings into a priority plan

Every issue is ranked by impact against effort. We agree together what gets fixed first, what gets scheduled, and what gets deliberately left alone. Nothing is changed inside your account until you have signed off on the plan.

03
Every week · The core loop

We prepare the weekly report

Each week we pull the data, analyse what moved and why, and prepare a clear report covering ACoS, TACoS, ROAS, spend trend, search term performance, listing changes, and any account health issues that have surfaced. No jargon without explanation. No vanity metrics.

04
Every week · Your call

You make the decisions

The report ends with recommendations, not surprises. Depending on the issues that surfaced that week, you decide the direction: push budget into what is working, pull it from what is not, escalate a case, or change the priority order. It stays your business and your account.

05
Every week · Execution

We implement accordingly

Once you have decided, we execute. Bids adjusted, keywords harvested and negated, listings updated, cases filed, shipments planned. Everything we change is documented so you have a clean record of what happened to your account and when.

Then it repeats. Report → your decision → implementation → report. Every week, without you having to chase anyone for an update. Once efficiency is stable, we scale spend into what is already proven to work rather than guessing.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Is the free audit actually free?
Yes, entirely. We go through your campaigns, listings, keywords, catalogue and account health and give you a written findings document. There is no obligation attached to it and no card required. If we are not the right fit for your business, you keep the audit and the analysis regardless. We do it because it is the fastest honest way for both sides to find out whether working together makes sense.
Which marketplaces and platforms do you cover?
Amazon is our primary discipline — US, UK, EU, Canada and Australia marketplaces. Alongside that we manage Walmart Seller Center and Walmart Connect, Shopify stores, eBay Seller Hub, TikTok Shop, and paid social through Meta and TikTok Ads. Most of our clients are on two or three of these and want one team handling them together rather than three separate vendors.
How quickly will I see results?
Wasted spend usually comes out in the first thirty days — that part is fast because it is mostly a matter of stopping money going to search terms that never convert. Structural improvement in ACoS typically stabilises between weeks four and eight, because campaigns need enough data to make bid decisions confidently. Organic rank improvement from listing work takes longer, usually one to three months. We will tell you which category your account falls into during the audit rather than promising a number we cannot control.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No. We work on rolling monthly retainers and defined-scope projects. If the work is not delivering, you should be able to leave without a penalty clause holding you in place. In practice, most engagements become long-term because the weekly reporting makes the value visible — but that is our problem to earn, not yours to be locked into.
Will I be handed off to a junior after signing?
No. The person who audits your account leads its management. There is no separate sales team, and no layer of account managers standing between you and the strategist making decisions inside your Seller Central. That is a deliberate constraint on how many accounts we take on at once.
What access do you need to my account?
For the audit, read-only user permissions in Seller Central and the Advertising Console are enough — we do not need or want anything more at that stage. For ongoing management we request the specific permissions required for the scope we have agreed, and nothing beyond it. We are happy to sign an NDA before any access is granted.
Do you work with new sellers?
Sometimes, if the expectations are realistic. Our process is built around diagnosing and fixing accounts that already have sales data to work with, because that is where our approach creates the most value. If you are pre-launch, we will tell you honestly whether you would be better served waiting until you have enough data for the analysis to mean anything.
What time zones do you work across?
We work flexibly and regularly overlap with US, UK and European business hours. Replies come within one business day as standard, and urgent account health issues are handled the same day they surface.
